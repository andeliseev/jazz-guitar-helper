# Changelog

What changed in each version of Jazz Guitar Helper. The newest is at the top; each release's notes on GitHub show the same list.

## Unreleased

- Chord tones comes first, before the drop types, and is where a new progression starts; the number keys follow the buttons: 1 Chord tones, 2 Drop 2, 3 Drop 3, 4 Drop 2&4.
- The fret numbers sit under every fretboard, Overlay's too, instead of in a row above them all.
- Frets, Height and Fit stay together when the toolbar wraps.
- The metronome always plays round and round, so Loop is gone, and Follow is off at first.
- Type the tempo, beside its slider.
- The click has sounds to choose (Tick, Wood, Hi-hat, Beep, or none) and its own volume, behind the click button in the metronome bar.
- A chord longer than a bar sounds again at each bar, and the staff writes it again in each bar.
- In Overlay with Follow, the next chord no longer flashes before it fades.
- A right-click opens no menu on sliders and lists either.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-17)

- Scales: turn them on, and each fretboard shows a scale to improvise over its chord, around the chord's notes, with the other notes named as tensions (9, 11, 13, ♭9…). Choose another over the fretboard; ready-made progressions start with the usual one for each chord's place in the key.
- Each chord has a length, from half a bar to four bars, at the end of its notes in the sidebar. In the ready-made ii–V–I and ii–V–i, the last chord lasts two bars.
- Staff: the progression above the fretboards, a bar every four beats, with each chord's pinned voicing (with Voice leading, the whole path), its notes, or its scale.
- Play: a metronome plays the progression round and round at the tempo you choose, with a bar of count-in; the chord playing stands out everywhere. Space starts and stops it.
- The chords sound as they play: held for their whole length by a pad to improvise over (Ambient, Warm pad, Strings or Organ), or plucked once, with reverb, volume and a bass note to choose, behind the sound button in the metronome bar.
- Fit, next to Height: the fretboards as tall as the window allows with all of them showing.
- Follow, in the metronome bar: while playing, only the chord playing and the next one, as tall as the window allows.
- A right-click no longer opens the web page's menu (Back, Reload, Print); text fields keep Cut, Copy and Paste.

## 0.1.2 (2026-11-17)

- Chord tones, next to Drop 2, Drop 3 and Drop 2&4 (or press 4): every note of each chord on the whole neck, without voicings. Your pinned voicings come back when you choose a drop type again.
- Chords with tensions keep every note: G7♯5♭9 is G B D♯ F A♭ in the sidebar, over its fretboard, in Chord tones and with All notes. It keeps the name you typed, and says what its drop voicings are: "voiced as G7♯5".

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-17)

- Suggestions as you type a chord: the chords that fit, with their names and usual short forms, and what you'll see for chords like Dm9 or G13. Choose one with the arrow keys and Enter or a click, or press Tab to keep typing.
- Quick add is now Presets, and a preset takes the place of your progression instead of adding to the end, with Undo. Point at one and click + to add it to the end.
- Save your own progressions under My presets: click one to bring it back, + to add it to the end, or delete it.
- Drag a chord in the progression to move it, instead of clicking arrows.
- Voice leading shows a path as soon as you turn it on, from the voicing you pinned last or else from the first chord. Turning it off now unpins the voicings it chose and keeps only yours.
- Frets moved from Settings to above the fretboards, next to Height.
- The chosen buttons above the fretboards no longer turn bold, so Drop 2&4 keeps its shape and the row no longer shifts.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-17)

- First version. Type a progression's chord symbols (`Dm7 G7 Cmaj7`, `B♭Δ`, `F♯ø`, `E♭°7`) or add a ready-made ii–V–I, ii–V–i, I–vi–ii–V or iii–VI–ii–V in any key, and see every Drop 2, Drop 3 or Drop 2&4 voicing of each chord on the neck at once.
- Choose the string sets and inversions to show, intervals or note names on the dots, and a fretboard per chord, lined up fret by fret and each with its fret numbers under it, or all chords on one fretboard. All notes adds every other note of each chord on the neck.
- Point at a voicing for its frets and inversion, and click it to pin it. With Voice leading on, the other chords follow with their closest voicings on the same strings.
- Fretboards as tall as you like (Height), light and dark look, a left-handed fretboard, and 12 to 24 frets. Your progression and choices are kept between launches.
- Settings links to the source code on GitHub, the website and the Support page.
- Updates itself: when a new version is out, Settings shows an Update button, and one click installs it.
