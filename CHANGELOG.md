# Changelog

What changed in each version of Jazz Guitar Helper. The newest is at the top; each release's notes on GitHub show the same list.

## Unreleased

- First version. Type a progression's chord symbols (`Dm7 G7 Cmaj7`, `B♭Δ`, `F♯ø`, `E♭°7`) or add a ready-made ii–V–I, ii–V–i, I–vi–ii–V or iii–VI–ii–V in any key, and see every Drop 2, Drop 3 or Drop 2&4 voicing of each chord on the neck at once.
- Choose the string sets and inversions to show, intervals or note names on the dots, and a fretboard per chord, lined up fret by fret and each with its fret numbers under it, or all chords on one fretboard. All notes adds every other note of each chord on the neck.
- Point at a voicing for its frets and inversion, and click it to pin it. With Voice leading on, the other chords follow with their closest voicings on the same strings.
- Fretboards as tall as you like (Height), light and dark look, a left-handed fretboard, and 12 to 24 frets. Your progression and choices are kept between launches.
- Settings links to the source code on GitHub, the website and the Support page.
- Updates itself: when a new version is out, Settings shows an Update button, and one click installs it.
